Who We Are

Profi Corporation —
Built on Trust

Since 1991, Profi Power has been operating at the intersection of industrial staffing and steel manufacturing. We understand the real demands of production environments — because we work in them every single day.

Our team works in Polish and Ukrainian, serving both local professionals and specialists from abroad who fuel Poland's industrial growth. We handle every aspect of employment: documentation, placement, onboarding, and support.

With two offices in Bydgoszcz and Nowy Sącz, we serve production plants across Poland — delivering certified welders and manufacturing staff faster than any agency in the region.
