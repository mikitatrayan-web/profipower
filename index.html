FAQ

Common Questions

What are the rules in Barcelona regarding cannabis?
Weed is decriminalized and you can smoke limited amounts of it in private properties and social clubs.
Can you carry weed in Barcelona?
Officially, it's illegal to carry, show, or consume weed in public in Barcelona. If you are low-key about it, no one is going to pat you down for a gram or two.
Can foreigners join and smoke weed in Barcelona?
Regardless of whether you are a foreigner or a local, you can access cannabis by becoming a member of a cannabis social club.
How do I get access to cannabis in Barcelona?
Access to cannabis is available through private social clubs. Although direct sales are forbidden, loopholes in the law allow people to pay for a membership.
Is membership free?
Yes, registration is totally free. You must be 18 or older and present a valid ID.
Where are you located and what are your opening hours?
We are located at Carrer d'Aragó, 209, L'Eixample, 08011 Barcelona. For current opening hours, please contact us via WhatsApp or Instagram.
